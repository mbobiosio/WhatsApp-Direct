## Privacy Policy for [Your App Name]

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]

### 1. Introduction

[Your Company/Developer Name] ("we," "our," or "us") operates the [Your App Name] mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Phone Numbers**: Users enter phone numbers to initiate WhatsApp conversations
- **Country Codes**: Users select country codes for phone number formatting

#### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected
- **Device Information**: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the App through Firebase Analytics
- **Crash Reports**: Error logs and crash data through Firebase Crashlytics

#### 2.3 Information Not Collected
- We do NOT store phone numbers entered by users
- We do NOT access or store your contacts
- We do NOT access your WhatsApp messages or conversations
- We do NOT collect personal identification information beyond what's listed above

### 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Facilitate WhatsApp communication by formatting and validating phone numbers
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Analyze app usage patterns through Firebase Analytics
- Identify and fix technical issues through Firebase Crashlytics
- Display app update notifications through Firebase Remote Config

### 4. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

- **WhatsApp**: Phone numbers are passed directly to WhatsApp. We do not control WhatsApp's use of this data. See [WhatsApp Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Firebase (Google)**: Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Firebase Analytics**: Collects usage data and device information
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Collects crash reports and error logs
- **Firebase Remote Config**: Manages app configuration remotely

### 5. Data Storage and Security

- Phone numbers entered are NOT stored on our servers
- Phone numbers are only passed directly to WhatsApp when you initiate a conversation
- We implement reasonable security measures to protect data collected through analytics and crash reporting
- Analytics data is stored according to Firebase's data retention policies

### 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information:
- With WhatsApp when you initiate a conversation
- With Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting purposes
- When required by law or to protect our rights

### 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access the information we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt-out of analytics data collection (through device settings)
- Withdraw consent for data processing

### 8. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

### 9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States where Firebase servers are located.

### 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

**Email:** [Your Email]
